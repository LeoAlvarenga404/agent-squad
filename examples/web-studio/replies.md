# Canned replies (owner's voice, short, with a next step) (EXAMPLE)

Answer the question asked, not the whole lecture. One answer and one next step. Numbers only from the catalog.

**How much is a website?** It depends on the size: a complete page starts at [floor for the origin], with the domain
in your name and everything live. And I'll build a free preview so you can see it before deciding. Want to see it?

**How long does it take?** Fast: with your photos and copy in hand, a few days. What usually delays things is the
content; I'll send you a short list of what I need.

**What's included?** The complete page, copy in your voice, showing up properly on Google (local SEO), a button that
opens a chat with you, domain and accounts in your name and a 30-day warranty for bugs in what I delivered. What is not
included I put in writing too.

**Do I have to pay every month?** No. The site is yours after setup. The monthly plan is optional: I take care of
hosting, make changes and send a report. Cancel whenever you want and the site stays yours.

**Is the site really mine?** Yours, in your name, in your account. If one day you want to go on without me, you take
everything.

**What do I need to send?** Not much: 3 good photos, the list of services and a paragraph about the business. I handle
the rest.

**Do you do logos / manage Instagram / ads?** Logos and social media are not my thing (I can refer someone good). I
build the site that turns visitors into messages in your chat.

**Will I be first on Google?** Nobody honest guarantees first place. The site comes out technically right so Google
understands you, and that gives you the best chance. Ranking depends on competition and time.

**How does payment work?** A down payment to book the slot and the rest when the site is ready and approved by you.
You never pay everything before seeing it. {{owner}} sends the payment details.

**What if something breaks after it's done?** A bug in what I delivered: fixed at no cost under the warranty. Something
new or a change of mind: we agree on it separately. With ongoing care, day-to-day changes are included (up to the plan
limit).

## Price objections
- **"That's expensive."** I get it. Expensive compared to what? Compared to a cheap template site, sure, and I can
  explain the difference. Tell me how much you expected to invest and I'll be honest about whether it can be done well
  for that. (Do not lower the price on the spot.)
- **"Someone else does it for much less."** They do, and for many people that's enough. The cheap ones are usually a
  template dozens of businesses use the same way. Yours is made for your business, and you own the domain and
  everything. If you just need to be online, that works. (Do not badmouth the competitor.)
- **"Can I pay in installments?"** Yes: half up front and half when it's approved and live. That way you never pay
  everything before seeing it.
- **"I'll do it myself with a site builder."** You can, and to start it's great. The question is time and results. If
  your time is worth more serving customers, that's where I come in.

## When it is not the right customer
"Thinking about it, I don't think I'm the right person for what you need right now: [direct reason]. I don't want to
take your money and deliver less than you need. If things change, get in touch." Saying no early is cheaper for both.

Price outside the range; a request outside the catalog (custom system, app, online store, logo, ads); questions about
invoices or contracts; complaints, billing, refunds; anything legal or health related; requests for another
customer's data; doubts about what the customer wants that change the price.
